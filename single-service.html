Authentic Ayurvedic Body Treatments & Rituals

Surrender your senses to our time-honored Ayurvedic body rituals, designed to cleanse, restore, and replenish your vitality from within. Using sacred Indian botanicals, cold-pressed therapeutic oils, and ancient exfoliation techniques, our treatments detoxify sluggish tissues, improve lymphatic drainage, and awaken your skin's natural, healthy radiance.

Why Choose This Service

Our signature body therapies unite classical Vedic wisdom with the pinnacle of modern spa luxury. Every session is customized to balance your individual Doshas (Vata, Pitta, Kapha), effectively releasing stored emotional tension, reducing chronic inflammation, and softening dehydrated skin.

We exclusively utilize hand-pounded herbal Ubtans, wild turmeric (Kasturi Manjal), pure Kashmiri saffron, and warm medicated oils sourced directly from Kerala's pristine organic plantations.

Working Process

Our sacred four-step body revitalization ritual begins with an in-depth Prakriti assessment, followed by a warm Abhyanga herbal massage to stimulate vital Marma points. Next, an exfoliating Ubtan body scrub gently sloughs off dead cells, concluding with an aromatic herbal steam (Swedana) and rose-petal hydrotherapy soak to seal in deep moisture and peace.

Post-treatment, our vaidyas offer a restorative warm Kashmiri saffron and cardamom tea while reviewing personalized homecare recommendations, balancing diet tips, and natural lifestyle practices to maintain your revitalized glow.

Fallspa is India's premier wellness sanctuary, uniting ancient Vedic wisdom, authentic Ayurvedic therapies, and luxury holistic care for complete rejuvenation of mind, body, and soul.
